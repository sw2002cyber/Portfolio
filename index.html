About

I started in marketing and advertising, running creative and campaign work across print, video, and digital storefronts. Along the way I kept finding myself more interested in the layer underneath the creative: what the data was actually saying, and why.

That's what brought me to Fordham for a dual master's in Information Technology and Business Analytics. Since starting the program, I've worked across Python, SQL, and Tableau to build things ranging from a client-sponsored recommendation platform to a sentiment-analysis pipeline on thousands of real customer reviews. I like projects that sit at the seam between a business question and a technical answer.

Originally from Buenos Aires, Argentina — which is also where, at fifteen, I ran a small slime business out of my house that ended up shipping nationwide. It's an early, admittedly strange, first lesson in both building an audience and delivering operationally once people actually show up.
